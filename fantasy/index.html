    How scoring works

    Career value. Each musician carries a value from their career in the graph, capped at their top three bands — a fourth band adds nothing to this part.

    Connection bonus. +4 for each pair among your five who played together in a band, up to +16. A fully connected band earns the full +16.

    Crowd-split. If many players pick the same musician, that musician is worth a little less to everyone who picked them — at half strength. Nobody is ever locked out of a pick. Crowd-split applies at settle, not in your provisional score.

    Winning. Beat the Gold line of 69 and the day counts as a win. Ten wins make Gold. Twenty-five make Platinum. Ties break on connection bonus first, then earliest submission.

    Free to play. No ads, no entry fee, no real money. Fantasy value never feeds back into the Six Degrees graph.
