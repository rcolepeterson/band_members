Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Six Degrees of Rock is a community-built map of rock music history. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect and what we do with it.

Information you give us

  • Account details. When you create an account, we store your email address and whatever name you provide. If you sign in with Google, Facebook, or Instagram, that provider shares your name, email address, and profile photo with us — only what’s needed to set up your account.
  • Things you add. Bands you submit, edits you make, bios you write, and game chains you share are stored so they can appear on the site.
  • Messages. If you contact us — for example through the sponsorship inquiry form — we keep your name, email, and message so we can reply.
  • Notifications. If you follow bands or opt into band-update emails, we use your email address to send them. You can turn these off at any time.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and request times) needed to operate, secure, and debug the site. Your sign-in session is stored on your own device so you stay signed in between visits.

How we use it

We use your information to run the site: keep you signed in, display the graph and your contributions, send the notifications you asked for, and respond to your messages. That’s it.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t share it with advertisers. We don’t use it for anything you didn’t ask for.

Service providers

A small number of providers process data on our behalf to keep the site running — currently our web host (Netlify) and our database provider (Neon). They only handle what’s necessary to operate the service.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and its data. Email sixdegreesofrock@gmail.com and we’ll take care of it. Deleting your account removes your profile; contributions you made to the public graph (band entries, edits) may remain as part of the shared map.

Children

Six Degrees of Rock isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date above and note it on the site.
