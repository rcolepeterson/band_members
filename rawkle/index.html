How to play Rawkle

Guess the band in 6 tries. One band a day — the same band for everyone. Every answer is a band that went Gold or Platinum. No obscure locals.

Read the shape first. The line under the title tells you the words and letters — 3 + 8 means a 3-letter word, a space, then 8 letters. Just type letters: the game jumps the spaces for you.

Fill every tile, then hit Enter. There is no guessing one word at a time — a guess only counts when the whole name is filled in. It doesn’t have to be a real band, but a real band that fits the shape is the smartest probe.

WHITE Gold = right letter, right spot. Blue = in the name, wrong spot. Dark = not in the name at all.

THE counts — The Hives play as THE HIVES, and the H is live in both words. Punctuation becomes a space: Guns N’ Roses plays as GUNS N ROSES. New band at midnight Pacific — win, and the band opens in the graph.
