How to play

Guess the band in 6 tries. One band a day — the same band for everyone. Every answer is a band that went Gold or Platinum. No obscure locals.

Type a guess that fits the tiles — same words, same spaces — and hit Enter. Punctuation doesn’t count: it becomes a space, so Guns N’ Roses plays as GUNS N ROSES. A guess counts if it fills every tile; it costs a try either way.

WHITE Gold = right letter, right spot. Blue = in the name, wrong spot. Dark = not in the name.

New band at midnight Pacific. Win, and the band opens in the graph — see who they’re connected to.
